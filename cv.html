Observing experience

60+ nights using the Himalayan Chandra Telescope. Principal investigator for HCT regular and target-of-opportunity proposals (2020–2024), GROWTH–India photometric follow-up (2022–2023), and the AstroSat Cycle A13 proposal X-ray Observation of Symbiotic Stars, with 45 ks of allocated observing time. Spectroscopic observations with HCT/VBO, photometric observations with GIT, and planning and coordinating target-of-opportunity observations.

Technical expertise

Spectroscopy & photometry

IRAF, PyRAF, Specutils, Astropy, Photutils; spectral extraction, flux and equivalent-width measurements, PSF photometry, and light-curve analysis. Time-resolved spectroscopy, Python-based reduction pipelines, spectral calibration and analysis, and variability studies of transient sources.

Programming & analysis

Python, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Astroquery, SciPy, IDL, and SQL; Lomb–Scargle periodograms, phase dispersion minimisation, emission-line diagnostics, spectral energy distribution and continuum modelling, and multi-wavelength analysis. Git and LaTeX; statistical modelling with SciPy and statistical analysis of time-domain datasets. Continuum modelling includes cool giant, pseudo-photosphere, and nebular emission components.

Machine learning

Scikit-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch; feature extraction and classification of astronomical time series, including light curves and stellar variability.
